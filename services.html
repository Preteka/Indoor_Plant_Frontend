FROM IDEA TO GREENERY

How Your GreenSpace Journey Works

We keep the process simple. Tell us what you need, let our team recommend the right solution, and we take care of everything from installation to ongoing maintenance.

01
ENQUIRY

Tell Us About Your Space

Share your workplace, event, location, approximate plant requirements, and preferred dates through our enquiry form.

02
ASSESSMENT

We Review Your Requirement

Our team reviews your space, plant requirements, rental duration, and service needs to determine the best approach.

03
CONSULTATION

Consultation or Site Visit

For larger or more detailed requirements, we arrange a consultation or site visit to understand the space properly.

04
QUOTATION

Receive Your Quote

We prepare a clear quotation based on the plants, planters, installation, rental period, maintenance, and other requirements.

05
APPROVAL

Approve & Pay

Once you approve the quotation, complete the required payment to confirm your booking according to agreed terms.

06
FULFILLMENT

We Deliver, Install & Care

Our team delivers and installs the plants, then provides the agreed maintenance and replacement support throughout the rental period.
