Our Origin

The Story Behind
GreenSpace Rentals

GreenSpace Rentals was founded with a simple observation: modern offices and venues wanted the uplifting beauty of live indoor plants, but purchasing them inevitably led to neglected, dying greenery, dead leaves on carpets, and wasted money.

We reinvented the entire model into a seamless rental subscription. Instead of buying, our clients rent acclimated, nursery-grade plants housed in designer sub-irrigated planters. Our certified horticultural specialists visit regularly to prune, water, feed, and monitor plant health metrics.
