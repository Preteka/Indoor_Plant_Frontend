Why Buying Plants Fails vs. Renting With Us

Eliminate upfront capital expense, dead plants, and messy maintenance with our managed botanical service.

The Problem with Buying

Traditional plant ownership pitfalls

  • Buying plants is expensive: Heavy upfront capital spent on delicate foliage and planters.
  • Maintenance takes valuable time: Office staff forget to water or drown roots, causing foul odors.
  • Plants become unhealthy: Inadequate light, pests, and dry HVAC air wither leaves within months.
  • Finding suitable plants is difficult: Retail nurseries lack species tested for low-light office floors.
  • Event greenery is temporary: Buying greenery for 1-day events leaves you with useless inventory.

The GreenSpace Advantage
