UNDERSTANDING YOUR QUOTE

What Goes Into Your Pricing?

Every space is different. Your final quote is based on the actual requirements of your workplace or event.

01

Number of Plants

More plants and larger quantities affect the overall rental requirement.

02

Plant Size

Large statement plants require different handling and space than smaller desk plants.

03

Planter Selection

Standard and designer planters can have different costs.

04

Installation

Delivery, placement, setup, and large-scale installation requirements can affect the quote.

05

Rental Duration

Office subscriptions and short-term event rentals are priced differently based on duration.

06

Maintenance

The frequency and level of ongoing plant care influence the service plan.
